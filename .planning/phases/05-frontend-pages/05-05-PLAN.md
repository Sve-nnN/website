<interfaces>
`Header` global (05-02): `logo` (upload), `navItems` (array of `link` groups), `ctaButton` (group: label/href). `Footer` global (05-02): `columns` (array: title + links array), `socialLinks` (array: platform/url), `legalLinks` (array: label/href), `copyrightText`.

`Authors` collection after 05-02's expansion: `name`, `jobTitle`, `bio`, `avatar`, `credentials` (array of `{label}`), `yearsExperience` (number), `socialLinks` (array: platform/url), `slug`. `AuthorByline` and `AuthorCard` must read all of these — `AuthorByline` shows a curated subset (avatar, name, jobTitle, first credential), `AuthorCard` shows everything.

The 53-image Cloudinary fallback pool lives at `https://res.cloudinary.com/dmufha3qv/image/upload/f_auto,q_auto/portfolio/fallback-image-{n}.avif` for `n` in 1..53 (per STATE.md's Phase 4 note: "JuanPortfolio's PostHero computes a deterministic per-slug fallback at render time... verified reachable"). Compute `n` via a stable string hash of the post's slug (e.g. sum of char codes, or a simple FNV-1a — must be deterministic across server restarts and locales, so do not use `Math.random` or a hash seeded by anything but the slug string itself) mod 53, then +1 to land in the 1-53 range.
</interfaces>
