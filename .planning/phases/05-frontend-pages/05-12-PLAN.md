| T-05-12-02 | Denial of Service | Unauthenticated public form, no rate limit | mitigate | Honeypot field drops obvious bot submissions; full rate-limiting is out of scope for a low-traffic personal-site contact form and is accepted as a residual risk consistent with this project's scale |
| T-05-12-03 | Tampering | Message body reflected into an email (HTML injection) | mitigate | Escape/sanitize the submitted `message` before interpolating into any HTML email body; plain-text email is an acceptable simpler alternative |
