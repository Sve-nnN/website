## Notable Incidents (for the historical record, both resolved)

1. **Phase 19:** A first attempt to apply the `CallToAction.richText` localization migration ran without a data backfill, causing real production data loss (Home's CTA copy, among others) — recovered via Neon point-in-time restore. Root-caused, migration corrected with a proper backfill-before-drop, re-applied with human review. Produced a lasting process improvement: the "Database Safety" rule in root `CLAUDE.md`, later relaxed by Juan (2026-07-12) to require explicit approval only for destructive operations (DROP/TRUNCATE/delete/lossy field reshapes), not additive/normal writes.
2. **Phase 21:** A real bug in `Header.navItems`' locale-write discipline (duplicate-id collision) briefly left the EN homepage showing a Spanish nav label. Found and fixed within the same phase, verified stable.

Both incidents are closed, documented in `.planning/STATE.md` and their respective `*-VERIFICATION.md` files, with no open action items.
